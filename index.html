01 / About

A little about me.

I'm a junior at the University of Washington's Paul G. Allen School, studying Computer Science with a minor in Business — a combo I picked because I can never quite resist the question, "okay, but how does this actually get used?"

My happiest projects sit at the intersection of technical depth and human impact: a fintech platform for community-driven lending, a data viz tool that surfaces traffic-safety patterns, a women-in-tech org I get to lead.

I love clean code, well-considered design, and the small moment when an idea finally clicks. Outside of class you'll find me running events, mentoring new CS students, or pretending the cherry blossoms outside my dorm aren't distracting me.

Safety First

An interactive data viz platform for exploring vehicle safety and traffic fatality data — designed to make hidden patterns finally visible.

  • Built visualizations with Vega-Lite and D3.js to reveal links between vehicle traits and fatality rates.
  • Implemented filtering, aggregation, and comparison so users can interrogate large datasets without writing a line of code.
  • Focused on accessible color, clear annotation, and a narrative that turns numbers into stories.
